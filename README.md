# LLNEXT_Bile_Acids_Microbiome_BAM

Bile Acid Composition in Lifelines NEXT in relation to microbiome and health 


This reposiorty consists of scripts used in the project: "Change in bile acid composition during pregnancy and its relationship with the maternal and infant gut microbiome"

### Overview 
Here, we present a comprehensive characterization of bile acids during pregnancy and cord blood 

📊 Largest longitudinal study (n = 264) demonstrating significant changes in maternal bile acid composition across normal pregnancy
🔄 Shift in bile acid conjugation, with increased overall conjugation and a preference for taurine over glycine
🧬 Decreased secondary bile acids, while gut microbiome composition and bile salt hydrolase (BSH) genes remain stable, suggesting altered enterohepatic circulation
🔗 Maternal–cord correlations show strong alignment in bile acid profiles, indicating maternal influence on neonatal metabolism
🦠 Cord blood bile acids linked to early microbiome colonization in infants
🌱 New insight into early-life programming, highlighting how maternal bile acids shape neonatal metabolic and microbial development
